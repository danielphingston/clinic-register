Privacy policy

Clinic Register · last updated 24 October 2026

Clinic Register is a free, open-source outpatient register built for one homeopathy clinic. This page explains what information the app uses, where it is kept and who can see it. The app has no server of its own: it runs entirely in your browser.

Google account data the app uses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions and nothing else:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile). Shown in the app's header and settings so you can see which account is signed in. They are kept in this browser only.
  • A private app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). The app stores its own data in Drive's hidden application-data folder so the clinic's devices stay in sync. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and other apps cannot see this folder.

Sign-in happens on Google's own page (accounts.google.com). The app never sees your Google password. It keeps only a short-lived access token in memory and does not request offline access.

Clinic records

Patients, visits, prescriptions, appointments, bills, stock and attachments that you enter are stored in two places only:

  • in this browser's storage (IndexedDB) on each device you use, and
  • in the private app folder of your own Google Drive, encrypted with your passphrase if you turn that on.

No copy is sent to the app's developer or to anyone else. The developer cannot read, recover or delete your records.

How Google user data is used and shared

  • Google data is used only to sign you in and to sync the clinic's own records between its devices.
  • It is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.
  • It is not used for advertising, profiling or to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person other than you (and whoever you share your Google account with) can read it.

Clinic Register's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

  • There are no ads, analytics, cookies for tracking or third-party trackers.
  • The app is served as static files by GitHub Pages, which may log basic request information (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Sending a reminder over WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Records stay until you delete them. Clearing this site's data in the browser removes the local copy.
  • To remove the Drive copy, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find Clinic Register and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • To revoke the app's access to your Google account, use myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out of the app also revokes its token.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the app's source repository. Questions or requests can be raised as an issue on GitHub.
